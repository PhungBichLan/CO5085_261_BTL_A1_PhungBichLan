1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Dataset Split

  • Source Code Access: ImageNet-100 Dataset
  • Selected Dataset: ImageNet-100 (100 classes extracted from the original ImageNet)
  • License: Apache license 2.0
  • Split Configuration:
    • Train set: 123,982 images (~96% of total images).
    • Validation set: 5,000 images (~4% of total images, fixed 50 images/class).
    • Test set: 5,000 images (~4% of total images, fixed 50 images/class).
    • Split ratio: Train : Validation : Test = 0.92 : 0.04 : 0.04
  • Reason for Selection: The dataset is sufficiently large (over 130,000 images) with 100 classes, balanced distribution, accurately reflecting the real performance of CNNs and Transformers without being limited by small datasets like MNIST or CIFAR-10.

  • Environment: PyTorch, GPU NVIDIA RTX 3500

  • Optimizer: Adam (lr(bb) = 0.0005, lr(head)=0.001)

6. Analysis

Classification Performance

ResNet50 with Freeze Full Backbone achieves the best performance among ResNet50 configurations, reaching 91.76% test accuracy and 0.9179 Macro F1-score. Partial fine-tuning, full fine-tuning, and training from scratch achieve progressively lower accuracy. ViT-B/16 outperforms all configurations, achieving 95.86% test accuracy and 0.95787 Macro F1-score.

Model Capacity and Training Efficiency

ViT-B/16 has 85.88M parameters, approximately 3.62 times more than ResNet50 (23.71M). However, its frozen-backbone configuration trains only 76,900 parameters, compared with 204,900 for ResNet50. ViT-B/16 requires longer training (13,570s) and testing (350.65s), while ResNet50 Freeze Full Backbone requires only 3,912.8s and 22.14s, respectively.

Training and Generalization

ViT-B/16 achieves the lowest training loss (0.164) and validation loss (0.3954), with a 5.16 percentage-point accuracy gap. ResNet50 Freeze Full Backbone also demonstrates good generalization, with a smaller 3.41 percentage-point gap. In contrast, full fine-tuning and training from scratch show weaker validation performance, suggesting that pretrained features are particularly beneficial under the current experimental settings.

Overall Analysis

ViT-B/16 provides the highest classification performance, while ResNet50 Freeze Full Backbone offers a more computationally efficient alternative. The results highlight that using pretrained weights and selecting an appropriate fine-tuning strategy are important for achieving effective classification and generalization.
